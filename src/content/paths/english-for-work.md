---
title: English for Work and Study
tagline: A daily, self-directed route to the English that opens jobs, universities — and every other resource on this site.
audience: Learners with basic English who need working fluency. Everything here is free and most of it needs no account.
outcome: Confident everyday listening and reading, workplace English, and an application-ready CV and cover letter.
effort: 20–30 minutes daily, ongoing
order: 4
stages:
  - title: Build the daily habit
    description: Language learning is won by consistency, not intensity. Pick your level on BBC Learning English and do one short lesson every day — the courses are graded from beginner to advanced so you always know what comes next.
    resources: [bbc-learning-english]
    checkpoint: Thirty consecutive days of lessons, logged in a simple journal with one new phrase per day.
  - title: Listening and speaking
    description: Train your ear with material made for learners — VOA reads real news slowly and clearly, with the text in front of you. Shadow the audio out loud — listen to a sentence, then say it back.
    resources: [voa-learning-english, bbc-learning-english]
    checkpoint: Record yourself giving a one-minute spoken summary of a news story. Repeat weekly and keep the recordings — hearing your own progress is the best motivation there is.
  - title: Read real things
    description: Graduate from learner materials to real books. Project Gutenberg has thousands of free classics — start with children's literature or short stories, where the language is simpler, and keep a vocabulary notebook.
    resources: [project-gutenberg, voa-learning-english]
    checkpoint: Finish one full book in English and write a half-page summary in your own words.
  - title: English for the workplace
    description: Emails, meetings, interviews and applications have their own conventions. BBC Learning English's business and workplace materials cover them directly.
    resources: [bbc-learning-english]
    checkpoint: A finished CV and cover letter in English for a real job or course you would actually apply to — reviewed by at least one other person.
